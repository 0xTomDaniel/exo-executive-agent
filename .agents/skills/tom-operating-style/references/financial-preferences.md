# Financial preferences

For Tom, keep business banking scoped to the explicitly approved legal entity;
never infer cross-company or personal-account authority. Resolve current account
identity and Keychain namespace from private runtime configuration.

No leverage, business-fund crypto speculation, or use of protected household,
tax, or runway capital. Prefer merchant-scoped single-use virtual cards with
the smallest sufficient cap for an approved purchase. These are Tom's preferences;
provider approval requirements and general credential safeguards belong to the
banking integration. Current balances, account identifiers, and dated financial
incidents belong in private memory, not this skill.
