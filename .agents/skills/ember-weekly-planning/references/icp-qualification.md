# ICP and Sales-Call Qualification

Use this reference when the Friday planning session turns to customer persona, customer-fit questions, sales-call targeting, or whether a prospect should count as high-signal.

## Boundary

Keep the **procedure** here. Keep the **current ICP, live qualification questions, pricing, call lists, and experiment state** in the user's note/task system, usually the relevant company/project note and current planning artifacts.

Do not freeze one week's ICP inside the skill unless the user explicitly says it has become durable procedure.

## ICP refinement workflow

1. Start from the current company/project note or weekly planning artifact.
2. Ask what evidence changed this week:
   - customer calls
   - demo reactions
   - repeated objections
   - willingness-to-pay signals
   - channel/source quality
3. Convert vague audiences into concrete qualification questions.
4. Define the threshold for:
   - prime sales target
   - high-signal discovery call
   - learning-only call
5. Use the threshold to choose next week's call list and decide what counts as success.

## Qualification-question pattern

Good qualification questions are mostly yes/no and map to real buying likelihood.

Prefer questions about:
- ownership or intent around the asset/workflow the product serves
- portfolio/company/team size or economic capacity
- frequency of the painful behavior
- complexity that makes current tools insufficient
- dissatisfaction with current tracking/control/outcome
- willingness to share data, connect accounts/wallets, or change workflow

Avoid questions that are merely demographic or flattering but do not change likelihood to buy.

## Scoring pattern

Use the live qualification questions to bucket prospects:

- **Prime sales target**: strong yes on most or all high-signal questions.
- **High-signal discovery**: enough yeses to plausibly buy, with one or two important unknowns.
- **Learning-only**: useful for language/channel insight but unlikely to buy now.
- **Not current ICP**: do not spend scarce sales-call slots unless strategically justified.

## Sales-call usage

Before scheduling or counting a call as high-signal:
- score the prospect against the live ICP questions
- name which unknown the call should resolve
- decide what evidence would change the persona, offer, artifact, or channel

During the call, listen for:
- repeated language around the pain
- proof that the pain is frequent or expensive
- willingness to provide access/context needed by the product
- willingness to pay or seriously discuss price
- clear objections that teach whether the persona, offer, artifact, or channel is wrong

## Aha-moment prompt

For each ICP, clarify what the customer actually buys:

- What painful job becomes easier or possible?
- What status, confidence, speed, or control do they gain?
- Why is this valuable enough to pay now?
- What moment in the demo/pitch should make them say, "Oh, I need this"?
